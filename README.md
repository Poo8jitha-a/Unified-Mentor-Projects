# 🛫 Bird Strike Analysis (2000–2011)

This project analyzes wildlife strike incidents reported by the **Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)** from **2000 to 2011**. The goal is to uncover key insights regarding the **frequency, severity, timing, and cost** of bird strikes affecting aircraft in the United States.

---

## 📌 Overview

* **Domain**: Aviation Safety & Environmental Impact
* **Data Source**: FAA Wildlife Strike Database (2000–2011)
* **Tools Used**: Excel / Power BI / Tableau (assumed)
* **Project Type**: Data Visualization & Exploratory Analysis

---

## 🎯 Goals

1. **Understand the patterns** and timing of bird strikes.
2. Identify **airlines, airports, and flight phases** most impacted.
3. Analyze **altitude and aircraft type** correlations.
4. Assess the **damage, cost, and injuries** resulting from wildlife strikes.
5. Evaluate the **role of pilot warning systems** and preventive measures.

---

## 🧾 Dataset Features

Each record includes:

* ✈️ **Aircraft Info**: Type, Make/Model, Number of Engines, Airline, Is Aircraft Large
* 🕑 **Flight Info**: Date, Altitude Bin, Phase of Flight, Origin State
* 🐦 **Wildlife Info**: Species, Size, Number Struck (Estimated/Actual)
* 💥 **Impact Info**: Indicated Damage, Effect on Flight, Injuries, Cost Incurred
* ☁️ **Conditions**: Sky Conditions, Precipitation, Pilot Warning, Remains Collected
* 📍 **Location**: Airport Name, Feet Above Ground

---

## 📊 Key Visual Analyses

* **Yearly Trends** in Bird Strikes (2000–2011)
* **Top 10 U.S. Airlines** with most bird strike incidents
* **Airports with highest number** of strikes
* **Total Financial Cost** incurred due to strikes
* **Seasonal & Monthly Analysis** of strike occurrences
* **Flight Phase Impact**: Take-off, Climb, Descent, Landing
* **Average Altitude per Phase** at time of strikes
* **Impact Severity** and Consequences
* **Altitude vs. Strike Effect Relationship**
* **Effect of Pilot Warnings** on strike outcomes

---

## ⚙️ Process

1. **Data Cleaning**: Removal of duplicates, handling missing values, converting units.
2. **Data Categorization**: Binning altitude, categorizing flight phases, seasons, etc.
3. **Visualization Design**: Clear, thematic charts and KPIs to support findings.
4. **Exploratory Insights**: Identifying patterns and outliers in cost, damage, and species impact.
5. **Interpretation**: Presenting insights in context of aviation safety improvements.

---

## ✅ Outcome

* Comprehensive understanding of when, where, and how bird strikes occur.
* Identification of vulnerable airlines and airports.
* Insights to guide aviation authorities in **risk mitigation strategies**, like improved warning systems and airport wildlife control.
